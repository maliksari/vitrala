Vitrala

A colouring book for iPhone and iPad. Nothing leaves your device.

HomePrivacy PolicySupport

Vitrala is a colouring book with forty hand-drawn ornamental pages, ten brushes, and a converter that turns your own photo into a page you can colour — entirely on your device.

No ads. No account. No tracking.

What it does

  • Forty pages across six categories: Animals, Nature, Sea, Landscape, Anime and Fantasy
  • Photo to line art, computed on your device and offline
  • Tap to fill, ten brushes, layers with blend modes, unlimited undo
  • Export up to print resolution, save to Photos, share in one tap
  • Backs up to your own iCloud account
  • Twelve languages, full VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type
