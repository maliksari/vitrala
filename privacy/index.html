Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Vitrala does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Your photos

When you turn a photo into a colouring page, the conversion runs on your device using a model bundled inside the app. Your photo is not uploaded, not transmitted, and not shared with anyone — including us. The feature works with the device in airplane mode, which is the simplest proof of this.

Vitrala asks for add-only access to your photo library, and only when you choose to save a page. That is the narrowest permission iOS offers: it lets the app write an image, and it does not allow the app to read your library.

Your drawings

Pages you colour are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they are copied to your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. That data lives in your private iCloud container, governed by Apple's terms; we have no access to it and no server of our own.

Turning iCloud Sync off stops the copying. Deleting the app removes the local copies; you can remove the iCloud copies from your device's iCloud settings.

Required-reason APIs

Apple asks apps to declare certain system APIs and why they are used. Vitrala declares two, both in its bundled privacy manifest:

  • File timestamps — to sort your saved pages by date.
  • User defaults — to remember your settings, such as the interface language.

Neither is used to identify you or your device.

Children

Vitrala is rated 4+ and is suitable for children. Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing to collect from a child either. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no external links inside the drawing experience, and no way for one user to send anything to another.

Third parties

Vitrala contains no third-party SDKs. Sharing a finished page opens the iOS share sheet, or Instagram if you specifically choose to send a page to Stories — at that point the receiving app's own privacy policy applies to what you sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/maliksari/vitrala/issues.
