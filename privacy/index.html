Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Vitrala collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your photos and drawings never leave your device except through your own iCloud account or a share you start. The only information that leaves the device is what is needed to handle an optional subscription, described below.

Your photos

When you turn a photo into a colouring page — including finding the main subject and removing the background, and picking a palette from its colours — all processing runs on your device using models built into the app and iOS. Your photo is not uploaded, not transmitted, and not shared with anyone, including us. These features work in airplane mode.

Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick and no access to the rest of your library. Vitrala asks for add-only access, and only when you choose to save a page to Photos.

Your drawings

Pages you colour are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they are copied to your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. Saved palettes are kept the same way, in your iCloud key-value storage. That data lives in your private iCloud container, governed by Apple's terms; we have no access to it and no server of our own.

Turning iCloud Sync off stops the copying. Deleting the app removes the local copies; you can remove the iCloud copies from your device's iCloud settings.

Time-lapse

While you colour, Vitrala can keep small snapshots of the page so you can export a sped-up video. They are stored only on your device, are not synced to iCloud, and are deleted with the page. You can turn recording off in Settings.

Subscriptions

Vitrala Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your name, e-mail address or payment details.

To check whether a subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and the App Store record of your purchases. This purchase history is not linked to your identity, is used only to unlock Premium features, and is never used for tracking or advertising. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Diagnostics

If the app crashes or runs slowly, iOS may give it an anonymous performance report (MetricKit). Vitrala keeps these reports on your device only. Nothing is sent unless you tap Share Diagnostics Report in Settings, which opens the share sheet so you decide where it goes.

Widgets and reminders

The home-screen widget and the optional daily reminder run entirely on your device. Your colouring streak is stored in the app's own container and shared only with Vitrala's widget. Reminders are local notifications; turning them on asks for notification permission, and they can be turned off at any time.

Required-reason APIs

Apple asks apps to declare certain system APIs and why they are used. Vitrala declares these in its bundled privacy manifest:

  • File timestamps — to sort your saved pages by date.
  • User defaults — to remember your settings, such as the interface language.
  • User defaults (app group) — to share your streak and the page of the day with Vitrala's own widget.

None of these is used to identify you or your device.

Children

Vitrala is rated 4+ and is suitable for children. It has no ads and no way for one user to send anything to another. Premium is an App Store subscription; on a child's device, Ask to Buy and Screen Time purchase controls apply.

Third parties

Vitrala includes one third-party SDK, RevenueCat, used only to verify subscriptions as described above. It contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Sharing a finished page opens the iOS share sheet, or Instagram if you choose to send a page to Stories — at that point the receiving app's own privacy policy applies to what you sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/maliksari/vitrala/issues.
