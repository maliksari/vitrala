Answers to the usual questions

Does the photo conversion send my picture anywhere?

No. It runs on your device, using a model inside the app. Turn on airplane mode and try it — it still works.

My colour spilled over the whole page.

The fill tool stops at closed outlines. On a page you made from your own photo, a line may have a gap in it, and paint escapes through the gap. Undo, then draw a short stroke across the gap with the pencil and fill again.

Where are my saved pages?

In My Work, on the gallery screen. If you turn on iCloud Sync they also appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Sync needs a network connection and an iCloud account with space left.

How do I change the app's language?

Settings → Language. The app opens in English by default; choose Device language to follow your system setting instead.

Can I print a page?

Yes. Export at Print quality — the page is resampled to 4096 px, which prints cleanly at A4 and A3.

I use VoiceOver.

The whole app is labelled, including the canvas, which reports how much of the page you have coloured. If something is unlabelled or reads wrongly, that is a bug worth reporting — please do.
